The lab

What the lab is building.

Sensor Scenario Studio (S³) is the lab's platform for radar and multi-spectral simulation. It builds scenarios on real terrain and maps from public data, runs radar and infrared sensors over them, and keeps every processing stage open for inspection.

  • 3D scenes of real places, with roads, buildings and land cover.
  • Coherent radar processing from pulses to tracks, and a four-face phased array.
  • An infrared camera co-registered with the radar.
  • Requirements traced to automated tests.

Explore Sensor Scenario Studio

How the work is done

S³ is educational and unclassified. Its scenarios are fictional, built on public geographic data and generic sensor models.
