Founder & Lab

A founder-led practice and the lab behind S³.

Chatterguru Consulting LLC brings systems engineering and sensor experience to defense and technology teams, and builds Sensor Scenario Studio as its own research platform.

CJ

Chatterpaul Joseph

Founder and principal systems engineer

Chatterpaul is a systems engineer who has spent more than twenty years on sensors for U.S. defense and homeland security programs: sonar, radar seekers, airborne electronic warfare, satellite communications, infrared missile warning and phased-array radar. That work has served the Navy, Army, Air Force and Space Force, and now the Department of Homeland Security.

He was born in Guyana and grew up in Pawtucket, Rhode Island. He founded Chatterguru in 2022, and the practice is based in Rockville, Maryland, near Washington, DC. Along the way he has led small engineering teams and mentored junior engineers and high-school students.

  3. 2024 to today

  4. 2022 to 2024

    Satellite communications and missile warning

    General Dynamics Mission Systems · Chatterguru's first engagement

    Chatterguru began here. Chatterpaul founded the company to work corp-to-corp as an independent contractor, and spent two years as a senior principal systems engineer supporting General Dynamics Mission Systems on two ground systems.

    MUOS, the military's narrowband satellite communications system: terminal and broadcast-service design, link-budget trade studies and an end-to-end communications model.

    Tranche 1 Tracking Layer, the Space Development Agency's infrared missile-warning satellites in low Earth orbit: tracking specialist for mission data processing, and a prototype global sensor-coverage map for operators.

  5. 2016 to 2022

    Airborne electronic warfare

    Northrop Grumman · Lead System Architect

    Led the architecture of RF electronic warfare systems for aircraft, guiding engineers across software, firmware and test. He used model-based systems engineering to connect requirements, interfaces and verification, and led an end-to-end digital twin that let the team assess a design before hardware was built. He also worked on RF links and payload concepts for satellites.

  6. 2008 to 2016

    Radar seekers and interceptors

    Raytheon Missile Systems · System Algorithm Development and Production Lead

    Developed and tested radar-seeker algorithms for detection, tracking and target recognition, and the flight simulations that predict a seeker's performance before a test and are checked against it afterwards. He worked on Small Diameter Bomb II and was the radar modeler for AI3, an interceptor built to defend against rockets, artillery and mortars, where he also developed the jam-resistant datalink from the ground radar to the interceptors. He supported more than 24 flight-test missions.

    In production, he took over the factory calibration routines and used design of experiments to raise yield by more than 30 percent, worth over $2 million a year.

  7. Early years

    Sonar and signal processing

    Graduate work at the University of Rhode Island on statistical signal processing for sonar, where he also taught MATLAB, led to a first engineering job on Navy submarine and torpedo sonar systems.

The lab

What the lab is building.

Sensor Scenario Studio (S³) is the lab's platform for radar and multi-spectral simulation. It supports fictional scenes, optional public map context and inspection of recorded sensor results.

  • 3D scenes of real places, with roads, buildings and land cover.
  • Coherent radar processing from pulses to tracks, and a four-face phased array.
  • An infrared camera co-registered with the radar.
  • Requirements traced to automated tests.

Explore Sensor Scenario Studio

How the work is done

S³ is educational and unclassified. Its scenarios are fictional and use generic sensor models. It is developed independently of any employer or customer program, from public sources only.
