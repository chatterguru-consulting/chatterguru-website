AESA maritime surveillance, Chesapeake Beach, Maryland: data sources and credits

This is the S³ Workbench Cesium Scenario Studio exported for the public website. The scene, target motion and sensor overlays come from the Workbench export at source revision 9853635cdc91, and your browser draws them. The sensors are simulated with generic, educational models, and the targets and missions are fictional.
