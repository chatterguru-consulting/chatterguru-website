South Mountain I-70, Maryland: data sources and credits

This is the S³ Workbench Cesium Scenario Studio exported for the public website. The scene, target motion and sensor overlays come from the Workbench export at source revision f853e8961db6, and your browser draws them. The sensors are simulated with generic, educational models, and the targets and missions are fictional.
